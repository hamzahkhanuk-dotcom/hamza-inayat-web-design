Our story

A kitchen built
around one fire.

Ember & Oak started with a simple idea: cook everything over real wood, buy from people we trust, and change the menu whenever the seasons do.

Our chefs work with small farms in Kent and Essex, our fish comes in from Cornwall every morning, and our wine list is short, natural and chosen by people who actually drink it.
