(06) FAQGood questions
How long does a website take?

Most landing pages take a few days, and a standard business website usually takes around one to two weeks depending on the number of pages and how quickly content is ready. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your business name, logo (if you have one), the pages you need, your text and photos (or I can use placeholders and suggest copy), and a few websites you like the look of.

Can I update the website myself afterwards?

Yes. WordPress and Shopify sites are easy to edit, and I'll show you how. For custom-built sites I can make updates for you, or hand over the files.

Do you handle hosting and domains?

I'll help you choose and set up hosting and connect your domain. Hosting and domain fees are paid directly to the provider and belong to you.

How do payments work?

Package orders are placed and paid through Fiverr. For direct custom projects, payment is made securely online by card (processed by Stripe) or PayPal — usually a deposit to start and the balance before final handover. See the terms below for details.

Do you work with clients outside the UK?

Absolutely. I'm based in London and work with clients in the UK and worldwide — everything is handled online.
